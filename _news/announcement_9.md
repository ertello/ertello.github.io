The paper *Are Binarization Hierarchies Transferable Across Binary Problems? Evidence from the 0–1 Knapsack Problem* is now published in the journal Biomimetics (JCR Impact Factor 3.9, Q1). This is a collaborative work with Professor Broderick Crawford from the [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso](https://www.pucv.cl/), Chile.
