The paper *Beyond Bio-Inspired Algorithms: Using Bird Landing Dynamics to Design Adaptive Parameter Control in Metaheuristics* is now published in the journal Biomimetics (JCR Impact Factor 3.9, Q1). This is a collaborative work with Professor Broderick Crawford from the [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso](https://www.pucv.cl/), Chile.
