The paper *A double-stage heuristic algorithm for the antibandwidth maximization problem via the linear bottleneck assignment optimization* is now published in the journal Computers & Operations Research (JCR Impact Factor 4.6, Q1). This is a collaborative work with 
Jintong Ren \([Hohai University](https://www.hhu.edu.cn/)\), Jin-Kao Hao \([Université d'Angers](https://www.univ-angers.fr/)\), and Zequn Wei \([Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://english.bupt.edu.cn/)\).
