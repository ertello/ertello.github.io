The paper *Optimizing the two-dimensional bandwidth problem under the maximum norm using exact and heuristic approaches* is now published in the European Journal of Operational Research (JCR Impact Factor 7.0, Q1). This is a collaborative work with Sergio Cavero and Eduardo G. Pardo \([Universidad Rey Juan Carlos](https://www.urjc.es/)\), Eric Monfroy \([Université d'Angers](https://www.univ-angers.fr/)\) and Claudia Vasconcellos-Gaete \([Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso](https://www.pucv.cl/)\).
